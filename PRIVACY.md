# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** November 04, 2026  
**Version:** 1.1

This Privacy Policy explains how the developers of Rusa ("Licensor", "We", "Us", or "Our") handle information in connection with the Rusa software, a MongoDB database client ("Software"). It should be read together with the End User License Agreement (EULA).

Rusa is an independent, unofficial client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by MongoDB, Inc. "MongoDB" is a trademark of MongoDB, Inc., used here only to describe compatibility.

By using the Software, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. SUMMARY

The Software is a local database client. **It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with Licensor.** It contains no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or tracking technologies. Everything the Software does with your data happens on your own device or directly between your device and the MongoDB servers you choose to connect to.

## 2. INFORMATION THE SOFTWARE DOES NOT COLLECT

Licensor does not collect or receive:

- Your name, email address, or other contact details
- Your database contents, documents, schemas, or query results
- Your connection strings, host names, usernames, passwords, or credentials
- Device identifiers, IP addresses, usage statistics, or crash telemetry
- Cookies, advertising identifiers, or location data

The Software has no account system and does not "phone home" to Licensor.

## 3. DATA YOU ENTER (CONNECTIONS AND CREDENTIALS)

- Connection details such as URIs, host names, ports, usernames, and passwords that you enter are held **in memory only for the current session**. The Software does not write them to disk.
- Those details are used solely to establish a connection to the MongoDB server **you** specify, and to send queries and write operations to that server.
- Your credentials are transmitted **directly to the server you connect to**. They are not sent to Licensor or any other party.
- You are responsible for the security of the servers you connect to and for whom you authorize to access them.

## 4. DATA YOU VIEW, MODIFY, OR DELETE

- The Software reads, writes, and deletes data **on the MongoDB servers you connect to**, at your direction. Licensor never receives a copy of that data.
- Bundled sample datasets ship with the Software and are stored locally in memory; they contain no real personal data.
- Import and export features read and write files on your device or a location you choose. Those files remain under your control.

## 5. REPORT BUG FEATURE

- The "Report Bug" option opens a public issue tracker in your system web browser. It does not automatically attach any data.
- Any information you choose to submit there (for example, a description, logs, or screenshots) is **voluntary** and is posted publicly. Do not include credentials, personal data, or confidential database contents in a bug report.
- Once you leave the Software and use your browser, the privacy policy and terms of the website you visit (for example, the code-hosting provider) apply to that activity.

## 6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND SERVERS

- The Software communicates with MongoDB servers that **you** configure. Those servers and any hosting providers are operated by you or third parties, not by Licensor, and their privacy and security practices are their own.
- The Software does not integrate advertising networks, social plugins, or third-party analytics.

## 7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Software is a developer tool not directed to children, and Licensor does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## 8. DATA SECURITY

Because Licensor does not collect or store your data, there is no Licensor-held data to breach. The security of your data depends on your own environment, the security of the servers you connect to, and the practices of any third parties involved. You are responsible for maintaining backups as described in the EULA.

## 9. YOUR RIGHTS

Because Licensor does not collect personal data through the Software, there is no personal data held by Licensor to access, correct, or delete. If you believe Licensor holds information about you, contact us using the details below.

## 10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Licensor may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the Software after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
